# Privacy notice

## Account information

Citizen accounts store the person's name and unique Indian mobile number. The mobile number is the sign-in identifier and associates reports with the account. The number is not verified by SMS. New citizen accounts do not collect email addresses or government identity numbers. Existing staff email identifiers are retained only during migration to phone-number accounts.

## Complaint information

Reports may include category, optional description, a confirmed location pin, consent timestamp, and private photos. Photos and report details are visible only to people authorized for that complaint. This service is not an emergency service.

Retention periods and deletion procedures are pending owner approval. No retention period is claimed by this notice.
