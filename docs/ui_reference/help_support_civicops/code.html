Frequently asked questions

How do I verify if a city repair is actually fixed?

Once a supervisor approves field completion photos, your report moves to Ready to verify. Open the report in your Citizen Portal to accept the resolution or reopen it with notes.

What do the complaint status badges mean?

Submitted means received by intake; Under review means city triage; Assigned / In progress means a department crew has an active work order; Verified closed means confirmed resolved.

Can I submit a report without creating an account?

Public transparency maps are open to everyone, while creating a resident account lets you receive status notifications and verify completed repairs.
