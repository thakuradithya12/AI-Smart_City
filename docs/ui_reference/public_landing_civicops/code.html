How civic resolution works

Transparent accountability from initial report to verified closure.

1. Submit location & photo

Pin the exact street location and attach a photo so crews know what to bring.

2. Routed to department

City coordinators assign the responsible department and dispatch a field work order.

3. Field repair & evidence

Field officers complete the repair and upload before-and-after photo proof.

4. Verified resolved

Supervisors audit the repair and residents confirm the issue is resolved.
