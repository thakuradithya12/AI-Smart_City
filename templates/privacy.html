Privacy notice

This notice explains what AI Smart City stores to receive and process civic reports.

Information we use

Citizen accounts use your name and Indian mobile number. The number is not verified by SMS. We do not ask for a government ID. A report may include its category, description, landmark, confirmed map pin, consent record and photos. Staff accounts may retain an email address during phone migration.

How we use reports

Authorised reviewers and service departments use report details to route and complete civic work. Photos are stored privately. The platform is not an emergency service.

Consent version

Current notice version: {{ privacy_notice_version }}. We record the notice version and time when you submit a report.

Retention

  • Verified and closed complaint details and photos: 12 months after closure.
  • Rejected or cancelled complaint details and photos: 6 months after that decision.
  • Inactive accounts: 24 months.
  • Deletion requests: processed within 30 days. We anonymise the account and retain append-only audit and status history.

When complaint retention expires, we remove its description, landmark, precise map pin and evidence files. The status-history and audit rows stay for traceability. Earlier audit records may retain message text that was recorded as part of an action. These periods are owner-provided implementation defaults, not legal advice.
